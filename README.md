ClimateSafe is a desktop-based application that helps users assess their
preparedness for selected climate-related hazards, identify areas for
improvement, and take appropriate preparedness actions.

> ClimateSafe is a preparedness self-assessment tool, not a disaster
> prediction or official early-warning system.

## Background

Climate-related hazards can affect individuals and communities. However,
people may not know how prepared they are or which preparedness actions
should be prioritized.

ClimateSafe provides a simple process:

**Assess → Identify → Act → Track**

The project supports **Sustainable Development Goal 13 (Climate Action)**,
particularly Target 13.1 on resilience and adaptive capacity to
climate-related hazards and Target 13.3 on climate education and awareness.
[UN SDG 13](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal13)

## Objectives

- Assess individual preparedness for selected climate-related hazards.
- Identify preparedness aspects that need improvement.
- Provide personalized, rule-based recommendations.
- Provide actionable preparedness checklists.
- Track preparedness progress and assessment history.

## Supported Hazards

The initial version focuses on:

- Flood
- Extreme Weather
- Extreme Heat

Additional hazards are considered outside the initial project scope.

## Main Features

1. **Preparedness Assessment**  
   Users answer questions related to their preparedness.

2. **Preparedness Score**  
   The system calculates a score based on predefined assessment criteria.

3. **Weakness Identification**  
   The system identifies preparedness areas that require improvement.

4. **Personalized Recommendation**  
   Rule-based recommendations are generated based on assessment results.

5. **Preparedness Checklist**  
   Users can manage recommended preparedness actions.

6. **Progress Tracking**  
   Users can monitor completed preparedness actions.

7. **Assessment History**  
   Previous assessment results can be stored and reviewed.

8. **Educational Content**  
   Provides concise information about selected hazards and preparedness
   actions.
